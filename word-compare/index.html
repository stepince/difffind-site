Word Document Comparison — Compare Two DOCX Files with a Document Diff

Load two versions of a Word document and see exactly what changed. DiffFind shows both DOCX files as formatted text side by side and highlights every added, removed, and changed passage right in the document, so you can review a revised contract, policy, or proposal without reading it twice. Add optional AI analysis to understand what a revised clause means.

Why Word Document Comparison is useful

Use it to review contracts, leases, policies, proposals, and reports that get revised and passed around. Instead of rereading both versions, start from a highlighted view of what changed.

Repeated wording is handled correctly: when the same phrase appears several times, DiffFind highlights the exact occurrence that changed, not the first match.

Good to know: DiffFind compares the document text. Images, headers, footers, footnotes, and text boxes are not compared, tracked changes are compared as accepted, and older .doc files need to be saved as .docx first.

Need a different format? Try PDF Comparison, Excel Comparison, or Word Comparison, or Text Compare. To understand what a change means, see Semantic Diff or Semantic Search.

Think of it as a document diff for Word: the deterministic comparison shows every edit, and optional semantic analysis helps explain whether the meaning changed. See also AI document comparison.
