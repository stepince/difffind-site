Semantic Find — Search by Meaning

Ordinary keyword search requires you to know the words in a document. Semantic Find lets you search for an idea or concept, even when the text uses different wording.

Why Semantic Find is useful

Traditional Find

Searches for exact words or character sequences. Useful when you know the spelling of a term, identifier, or value.

Semantic Find

Searches for conceptually related content. Useful when you know the idea but not the exact wording.

Lexical search remains useful for exact matches. Semantic Find complements it for conceptual queries. To understand meaning across two versions, use Semantic Diff.
