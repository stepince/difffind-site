Keyword search and semantic search

Keyword search

Find exact words or character sequences. Useful for known terms, identifiers, and values.

Semantic Search

Find conceptually related content. Useful when you know the idea but not the document’s exact wording.

Semantic Search complements exact matching. Use it to locate a requirement, a description of behavior, or a policy provision, then read the original passage to confirm the details.

This feature searches within the document you provide. To examine changes between versions, use Text Compare or Semantic Diff or Semantic Search.

Semantic Find understands the intent of your search and can locate relevant content even when the document uses different terminology. It is AI semantic search for the text you load; to search whole documents such as PDFs, see semantic document search.

Direct answers, no AI required

Ask a question such as “who is the tenant?” or “what is the termination date?” and DiffFind can pull the answer straight out of your document, word for word. It ranks passages with on-device meaning-and-keyword scoring and copies the answer from the text. It does not write or paraphrase anything, and no external AI service is called or sent your document for this.

Grounded in the source

Each answer is backed by its source quote, with the page and section on hover. Click it to jump to and highlight the exact spot in the document.

Silent when unsure

Topic searches, facts the document does not state, and conflicting or “except / unless” wording show ranked passages and no answer. A missing answer is better than a wrong one.

It works across plain text, PDF text, JSON, YAML, XML, and spreadsheet data. If you connect an AI provider, the provider’s answer is used instead.
