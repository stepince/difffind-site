Excel Comparison — Compare Every Sheet

Load two versions of an Excel workbook and see exactly what changed. DiffFind compares every sheet of an XLS or XLSX file at once, so you can spot changed prices, quantities, and statuses without scrolling through cells.

Why Excel Comparison is useful

Use spreadsheet comparison to review product catalogs, budgets, price lists, inventory exports, and reports that get updated and passed around.

Instead of checking cells by eye, start from a highlighted view of what changed, across every sheet in the workbook.

Need to compare a document instead? Try PDF Comparison or Text Compare. To understand what a change means, see Semantic Diff.
