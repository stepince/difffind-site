Common questions

Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers about privacy, formats,
AI analysis, and the API.

Does DiffFind store my files?

Not by default. Text you enter on this page goes to the DiffFind app only when you click Find difference, and nothing is stored on this page. In the app, content submitted for comparison is processed to return a result and is not retained beyond what is needed to do so, unless you choose to save it, such as comparison history stored in your browser. See the privacy policy.

Which file formats does DiffFind support?

DiffFind compares plain text, JSON, YAML, XML, CSV, PDF, Excel spreadsheets (XLS and XLSX), and Word documents (DOCX). You can also load content from a URL or a cURL command.

What does semantic analysis do?

Semantic analysis is optional AI-based meaning analysis. It highlights material changes and explains their likely impact. Cited facts are checked against your source documents, and the verification status is shown.

Do I need AI to compare documents?

No. The core comparison is consistent and repeatable and works without AI. Semantic analysis is an optional layer you can add when you want to understand what a change means.

How detailed can a comparison be?

You can compare by character, word, sentence, line, or paragraph, and see additions, removals, and moved text with surrounding context.

How do I call the DiffFind API?

Send a POST request to /v1/compare with your before and after content. The full request format is described in the OpenAPI specification at app.difffind.com/openapi.json.
